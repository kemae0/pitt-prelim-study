# Pitt Prelim Study

A study app for the University of Pittsburgh mathematics Ph.D. preliminary exams in analysis and linear algebra. Designed for students with a solid undergraduate background who want to strengthen their understanding and proof-writing skills.

[Open Pitt Prelim Study](https://kemae.neocities.org/pitt-prelim-study/)

## Features

- 757 problems from exams spanning 2000–2026, with step-by-step hints and worked solutions.
- A guided curriculum with 70 theorem and definition lessons, including proofs.
- Flashcards and interactive mathematical visualizations.
- Original exam PDFs, searchable problems, and bookmarks.
- Progress saved in your browser, with export, import, and reset options. No account required.

Exams come from [Pitt’s sample preliminary exam archive](https://www.mathematics.pitt.edu/graduate/graduate-handbook/sample-preliminary-exams).
